The Perfect Swan

Long ago, in a magnificent palace, lived a powerful emperor named Zhang Wei.

One day, the emperor ordered a famous artist, Cheng Li, to paint a beautiful white swan.

The emperor waited. He became furious and bad-tempered because Cheng Li was very slow.

Finally, Cheng Li showed his painting. It was a perfect swan! The emperor was astonished. Cheng Li was not lazy; he had practised every day to make the perfect painting.
